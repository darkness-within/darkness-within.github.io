Privacy Policy

At Darkness Within Azeroth Adventures, protecting your personal privacy is a top priority. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is used, and how we safeguard it when you use our website.

1. Information Collection

We do not require you to provide personal information simply to browse our website. We may collect basic information only when you:

  • Contact us via a contact form (e.g., name/alias, email address).
  • Submit a membership application for the Guild.

2. Use of Information

Any data collected is strictly used to:

  • Improve the overall website user experience.

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to outside parties.

This website may use essential technical cookies to ensure proper site functionality. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the official website contact form or via our official Discord server.
